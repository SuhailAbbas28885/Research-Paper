## 🌌 PROJECT INTRODUCTION

> *"The past explains the present — but the future depends on what we do with it."*

This research explores the complex and historically significant relationship between **Pakistan 🇵🇰 and Bangladesh 🇧🇩**, moving beyond the conventional focus on the 1971 conflict to examine the possibilities of reconciliation, cooperation, and future engagement.

The study investigates the **political, economic, diplomatic, and social factors** shaping bilateral relations and identifies opportunities through which both countries can gradually move toward a more cooperative relationship.
